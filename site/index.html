How we calculate
  • Budget is total expenditure excluding debt repayment (revenue + capital outlay + loans & advances); tax is the state’s own tax revenue. Both are revised estimates (RE).
  • Schemes announced uses each state’s Social Welfare & Nutrition sector spend (RE), as printed in PRS’s budget analysis, until scheme-level totals are verified. States classify welfare spending differently (some book much of it under SC/ST/OBC welfare), so compare states with care.
  • Central Revenue and State revenue use one definition: Revenue Receipts, FY 2025-26 Budget Estimates, exactly as published, excluding borrowings and capital receipts. The Centre’s figure is from the Union Budget at a Glance (indiabudget.gov.in) and is net of states’ share of central taxes; each state’s figure includes that share. State figures are taken from PRS Legislative Research’s analysis of the state budget. “N/A” means no published figure.
  • Actually spent / collected on a state’s page comes from CAG’s provisional accounts (March report, full year). It is compared with the revised estimate only when both use the same definition, year and accounting heads; otherwise the comparison shows N/A.
  • Schemes are discovered on myScheme and the National Portal of India and in national and regional-language news, then matched against budget documents, departmental reports and notifications. Where a news figure and the government figure differ, the government figure is shown. A scheme seen only in the news is listed as “Reported — awaiting official verification”, with no amount, and is never added to totals.
  • Each scheme is marked as the state’s own (“State scheme”), “Centrally sponsored” (cost shared by Centre and state) or a “Central scheme”, from myScheme and the Union Budget’s list of centrally sponsored schemes; it is left unmarked when the sources don’t say.
  • “Budget docs (via PRS)” figures come from PRS Legislative Research’s analysis of the official budget and become “Verified” once matched in the government document itself.

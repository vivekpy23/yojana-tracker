How we calculate
  • Budget is total expenditure excluding debt repayment (revenue + capital outlay + loans & advances); tax is the state’s own tax revenue. Both are revised estimates (RE).
  • Schemes announced uses each state’s Social Welfare & Nutrition sector spend (RE), as printed in PRS’s budget analysis, until scheme-level totals are verified. States classify welfare spending differently (some book much of it under SC/ST/OBC welfare), so compare states with care.
  • Benefits disbursed shows only amounts actually transferred, as published by a government source with its reporting period and date. “Partial” means only some of the state’s schemes have a published figure. It is never estimated from budget allocations; “—” means no reliable figure is published yet.
  • Actually spent / collected on a state’s page comes from CAG’s provisional accounts (March report, full year). It is compared with the revised estimate only when both use the same definition, year and accounting heads; otherwise the comparison shows N/A.
  • Schemes are found in PIB and national news, then matched against government documents. Where the two differ, the government figure is shown. News-only figures are marked “Unverified” and never added to totals.
  • “Budget docs (via PRS)” figures come from PRS Legislative Research’s analysis of the official budget and become “Verified” once matched in the government document itself.
